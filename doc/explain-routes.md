# explain Route types

## Fastest

- Direct walking route.
- No scenic stops.
- Best when you want speed.

## Scenic

- Personalized route with up to 3 stops.
- Good balance of interesting + not too long.

## Scenic+

- More exploratory route with up to 4 stops.
- Usually more discovery than Scenic.

## Which one should I pick?

- In a rush: pick `Fastest`.
- Normal walk: pick `Scenic`.
- Explore more: pick `Scenic+`.
