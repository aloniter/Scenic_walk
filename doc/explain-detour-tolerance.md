# explain Detour tolerance

## What is this?

- It controls how far the app can look away from your direct path.

## Levels

- `Low`: close to your path (about 200m).
- `Medium`: balanced (about 400m).
- `High`: wider search (about 700m).

## What changes?

- Higher detour can find more interesting places.
- Lower detour keeps the walk more direct.

## Simple rule

- Want more variety? choose `High`.
- Want faster walking? choose `Low`.
