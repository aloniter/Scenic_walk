# explain Max extra time

## What is this?

- This is your time budget above the fastest route.

## Example

- Fastest route is 30 minutes.
- Max extra time is `+10`.
- Scenic route should stay at 40 minutes or less.

## What happens if route is too long?

- The app removes weaker scenic stops.
- Then it recalculates until the route fits your budget.

## Simple rule

- Bigger number = more chance to keep more stops.
- Smaller number = stricter and faster route.
