How to tune your route

Categories

  • Sea: Routes that try to pass near the promenade, shoreline, or beach viewpoints.
  • Instagram & Art: Photogenic streets, viewpoints, galleries, murals, and public art zones.
  • History & Architecture: Museums, historic landmarks, and notable buildings with strong cultural context.
  • Main Streets: Lively boulevards, busy shopping streets, and central city zones.
  • Food & Markets: Well-rated cafes, bakeries, restaurants, market food spots, and nearby market streets.
  • Chill: Calmer paths, greener areas, shaded streets, and a slower pace.

Detour tolerance

  • Low: Small detours that stay close to the fastest walk.
  • Medium: Balanced detours with more highlights while keeping the route efficient.
  • High: Bigger detours for a richer walk, usually with more extra time.

Max extra time

This caps how many minutes above the fastest route your scenic options are allowed to add.

Open now

When enabled, we prioritize places that are open right now, which can narrow the available options.

How routes are picked

We start from the fastest route, look for strong nearby matches to your selections, then build options that balance quality and efficiency.

What you get

You get 3 options: Fastest (direct), Scenic (balanced highlights), and Scenic+ (more exploration). For best results, pick 2-3 categories and keep detour on Medium or High.
